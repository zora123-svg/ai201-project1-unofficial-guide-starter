About University of St. Thomas

UST is a Catholic University with a beautiful, neo-gothic style campus in St. Paul. The institution emphasizes small classes and attentive professors. Students are described as on the wealthy side, but also very friendly and down to earth. The party scene mostly revolves around numerous bars close to campus and in Downtown Minneapolis. Sports and Greek life are minimal aspects of campus culture.

Rental Market Information

Average rent prices in St. Paul: studio about $1,130/month; 1-bedroom about $1,350/month; 2-bedroom about $1,680/month.

Search timeline: Students typically begin searching in November, with most leases signed by June due to St. Paul's strict ordinances on student housing.
